Assignment 3

Factorial, Sum, and Average

Three separate calculators for the first N natural numbers. Each one is solved with a specific loop structure rather than a mathematical shortcut.

A. Factorial while loop

Computes N! by multiplying 1 × 2 × … × N.

Allowed range: 0 to 170 (0! equals 1).

B. Sum do…while loop

Adds up 1 + 2 + … + N.

Allowed range: 1 to 1,000,000.

C. Average for loop

Totals the first N natural numbers, then divides the total by N.

Allowed range: 1 to 1,000,000.
